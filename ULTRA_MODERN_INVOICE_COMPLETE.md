## 🧪 Testing Instructions

1. **Open the Web App**: Navigate to http://localhost:3000
2. **Set Company Info** (Optional):
   - Click ⚙️ Settings button
   - Upload company logo
   - Fill in company name, address, email, phone
   - Click Save
3. **Generate Invoice**:
   - Click the "Invoice" card
   - Fill in customization form:
     - Client Name: "Acme Corp"
     - Items: "Logo Design"
     - Quantities: "1"
     - Prices: "55"
     - Payment Method: "Cash"
     - Note: "Thanks for your business!"
   - Click "Generate Document"
4. **View Result**:
   - Preview modal will show the ultra-modern design
   - Click "Download Document" to save as .txt file
5. **Test Fraud Detection**:
   - Click "Scan for Fraud" in preview modal
   - View security analysis with forensic checks
